
==================================================
[AQEEDAH] Belief in Allah
Arabic: أن تؤمن بأن الله هو الخالق الرازق المدبر لكل شيء، وأنه الواحد الأحد الفرد الصمد
English: To believe that Allah is the Creator, Provider, and Controller of all things, and that He is One, Unique, Eternal, and Self-Sufficient.
Luganda: Okukkiriza nti Allah ye Mutonde, Omuyambi, n'Omulamuzi w'ebintu byonna, era nti Yye Omu, Taliiko mufaanana, Omulamu olw'ensi, so talina ky'ayagala.
